# Glossary — ch16-staff-and-payroll.md

- **employees** — Employee: the person record, shared across organizations.
- **employee_org_location_assignments** — Assignment: an employee's job at one organization and location, with role and staff login.
- **employee_schedules** — Weekly working hours of an employee at one location.
- **employee_leaves** — Leave request.
- **doctor_availability_overrides** — Availability exception for one date at one location.
- **payroll_records** — Payroll record: one employee, one location, one month.
- **Portal access** — `has_portal_access` on an assignment: whether the person may log in at that location.
