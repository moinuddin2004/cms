# Delete your account

> DRAFT for the owner and a lawyer to review.

This page explains how to delete your [APP_NAME] account. It is the public deletion URL given to Google Play.

## Patients: delete inside the app

1. Open [APP_NAME] and sign in to the patient space.
2. Go to **Account**, then **Delete account**.
3. Read what will be deleted, type **DELETE** and confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your app account and sign-in details (email, Google link)
- All profiles you created (yourself and family members)
- Medicine reminders and dose history
- Connections to clinics (links) and your push notification devices
- Your sessions on every phone

## What is not deleted

- Records your clinic holds about you (visits, prescriptions, invoices). The clinic owns them and keeps them under its own rules and medical record requirements. Ask the clinic if you want them corrected or removed.
- A minimal access log that says an account (with no name or email) read a record, kept for security and clinic accountability.
- Backups, which are overwritten within [BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS] days.

## Cannot use the app, or you are clinic staff?

Staff accounts belong to the clinic: ask your clinic owner to remove your login. To ask us to delete a patient account or any personal data without using the app, email [CLINIC_PLATFORM_CONTACT] from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm your identity first and will reply within [RESPONSE_DAYS] days.
